What we do

Four offers. Each one backed by delivered work — not a capability slide.

AI-Augmented Infrastructure Residencies

A senior principal embedded in your team for defined weeks — backed by an AI delivery workforce, so every hour produces durable artifacts.

  • Senior engineering on-site or remote, in defined weekly blocks
  • AI agents produce documentation, runbooks and validation reports alongside the work
  • Every artifact versioned and handed over — the knowledge stays with you
  • Flexible scope across virtualization, hybrid platforms and migration

Delivered: a multi-site Hyper-V & SCVMM residency for a state-government end client, under a partner residency contract with a leading OEM.

Azure & Hybrid Platform Engineering (Design–Build)

Fixed-scope design, build, rebuild and migration of Microsoft virtualization and hybrid platforms — with a defined outcome, not open-ended consulting.

  • Azure Local, Hyper-V failover clustering, SCVMM, Network ATC
  • Target-state designs reviewed with your engineers before anything is built
  • Dry-run-safe automation with a rollback plan for every phase
  • Secure-by-design build standards in every artifact

Application Migration Factory

Application migration as a governed factory — not a heroic one-off project.

  • Built on a leading OEM's three-phase migration methodology
  • Phase gates with a human go/no-go decision at every transition
  • Generated runbooks and generated rollback plans for every move group
  • Full traceability: what was done, when, by whom, on whose approval

Productized inside an active application-migration engagement with a leading global OEM.

How we work

  1. Assessment

    It starts with a 30-minute call — no slides, just your environment and your goal. If there's a fit, we follow up with a fixed-scope assessment or design sprint (two to four weeks) that sizes the work properly. You get a decision-ready picture of your estate, not a sales document.

  2. Engagement

    Choose the model that fits: a residency (multi-week blocks of senior hours), a fixed-scope design–build with a defined statement of work, or an advisory retainer. We also deliver under partner and channel contracts — both of our delivered engagements ran under primes, and we're comfortable being the engine behind yours.

  3. Operate

    Handover is the point, not an afterthought. Designs, runbooks, rollback plans and documentation are versioned and yours. We enable your team to run the platform — and stay in reach on retainer if you want us there.

Engagement models: Residency · Fixed-scope assessment / design sprint · Design–build · Advisory retainer · Partner & channel delivery
